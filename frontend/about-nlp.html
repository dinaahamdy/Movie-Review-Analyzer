Understanding Natural Language Processing

Welcome to the educational core of MovieMind! This project demonstrates how machines attempt to understand human emotion through text.

What is NLP?

Natural Language Processing (NLP) is a branch of artificial intelligence that gives computers the ability to read, understand, and derive meaning from human languages.

What is Sentiment Analysis?

Sentiment Analysis is an NLP technique used to determine whether data (like a movie review) is positive, negative, or neutral. In a real-world scenario, this is often done using complex Machine Learning models trained on thousands of texts.

How MovieMind Works (The Baseline)

For this academic frontend project, we implemented a Lexicon-based Baseline Classifier in Vanilla JavaScript. It uses predefined lists of positive and negative words and performs a basic mathematical calculation on the review text.

Limitations of our Baseline

While functional, a simple word-matching approach has major limitations compared to deep-learning models:

  • Sarcasm: "Oh great, another terrible movie." The word "great" might trick the classifier into registering positive sentiment.
  • Context: "The villain was horribly evil." (A compliment to the acting, but uses a negative word).
  • Nuance: Complex mixed opinions can confuse a basic word-counter, often resulting in a "Neutral" score.
